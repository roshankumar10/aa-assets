Our Story

Technology is moving faster than ever, but tech education for young people hasn’t kept up. The gap is structural: demand for engineers far outpaces what schools can staff, so most students never learn from a working engineer before they choose what to study.

Atlas Academy was built to close that gap. Engineers and designers from EA, IBM and Confluent teach small classes in Richmond and Vancouver, turning young curiosity into real creative and technical skill.

What will they actually build?

Software Engineering

Students program a Raspberry Pi in Python, from their first blinking LED to the Plant Monitor: light, temperature, humidity and soil sensors reporting on a real plant. By Level 6 they build their own AI agent from the ground up.

3D Modelling & Animation

In Blender, the studio-grade tool used across film and games, students model, rig, light and animate their own original characters and environments.
