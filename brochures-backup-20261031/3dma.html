Won’t AI just do this?

The artists who direct the work are the ones who understand how it’s built. Our students learn the craft underneath: modelling, rigging, lighting and animation in Blender, the tool used across film and games.

Who teaches

Designers and engineers working in the industry today, at EA, IBM and Confluent. Classes stay small, so every student gets real feedback on their work.

Is 3D Modelling & Animation right for my child?

Sharpens spatial awareness and math skills

Designing objects in a digital space trains visual thinking and depth perception, giving them a real head start in math, architecture and design.

Patience and attention to detail

A 3D model only looks right when all the small pieces line up. As kids adjust curves, align edges and smooth surfaces, they practise slowing down and checking their work.

Deepens how they observe the real world

This “way of seeing” shifts how they view their surroundings, turning everyday curiosity into an eye for form, structure and physics.
