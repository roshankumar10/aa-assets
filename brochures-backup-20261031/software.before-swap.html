About Atlas

Atlas Academy is a technology academy for kids and teens in Richmond and Vancouver. Every course is designed and taught by engineers and designers working in the industry today, at companies like EA, IBM and Confluent.

Classes are small, so every student gets real time with their instructor. There is no copying steps off a screen: students learn why something works, then build it themselves.

Won’t AI just do this?

The kids who direct AI best are the ones who understand how it’s built. That’s why our students start with real code on real hardware, and by Level 6 build their own AI agent from the ground up.

Who teaches

Engineers working in the industry today, at EA, IBM and Confluent. Classes stay small, so every student gets real time with them.

The Atlas team. Engineers and designers from EA, IBM and Confluent.

Is Software Engineering right for my child?

“My kid isn’t a tech kid.”

They don’t need to be. Level 1 starts with lighting a single LED.

Grit and resilience

When a sensor misses a reading, they figure out why. That’s debugging, and it’s resilience.

Critical thinking

To build the Plant Monitor, students decide what each sensor should measure, how often, and what counts as a warning. One big problem, broken into small steps.

Self-directed learning

No copying steps off a screen. At Level 5, students design how their own social network stores accounts, posts and friendships.

What will they actually build?

  • L1
    Intro to Software Engineering LEDs, buttons and buzzers on a Raspberry Pi, in Python.
  • L2
    Interactive Programming and Sensors The Plant Monitor: light, temperature, humidity and soil sensors.
  • L3
    Solving Problems with Algorithms Sorting, grids and objects in their first big project.
  • L4
    Web and Internet Foundations How the internet works, and building for the web.
  • L5
    The Backend Their own social network: accounts, a database and an API.
  • L6
    Advanced & Emerging Technologies Their own AI agent, built from the ground up.
