About Atlas

Atlas Academy is a technology academy for kids and teens in Richmond and Vancouver. Every course is designed and taught by engineers and designers working in the industry today, at companies like EA, IBM and Confluent.

Classes are small, so every student gets real time with their instructor. There is no copying steps off a screen: students learn why something works, then build it themselves.

Is 3D Modelling & Animation right for my child?

Sharpens spatial awareness and math skills

Designing objects in a digital space trains visual thinking and depth perception, giving them a real head start in math, architecture and design.

Patience and attention to detail

A 3D model only looks right when all the small pieces line up. As kids adjust curves, align edges and smooth surfaces, they practise slowing down and checking their work.

Deepens how they observe the real world

This “way of seeing” shifts how they view their surroundings, turning everyday curiosity into an eye for form, structure and physics.

Something real to show for it

Every level ends with a finished piece: a scene, a rigged character, a shot with real VFX. Work they can share with family and friends, and start a portfolio with.

No art experience needed

Level 1 starts from the basics of Blender. If your child loves games, films or drawing, that’s all the start they need.
